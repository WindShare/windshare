|---|---|---|

## Intentionally retained boundaries

- `SelectionObservationV1` is a diagnostic digest, not output or resume authority.
- Native and IndexedDB legacy cleaners remain as isolated ownership-scoped maintenance code. They do not migrate or
  import old ranges, and they never delete published files.
- Root first-level prefetch, the 0/8-second connection policy, ZIP member irreversibility, and `LiveOnly` sender
  shutdown semantics are unchanged.
- Release and module-publishing evidence belongs to the existing gates described in
  [validation.md](validation.md); this historical plan does not repeat transient CI results or claim a release.
