# Payment assets

The three PNGs are the supplied Alipay, OKX Pay and Binance Pay QR codes, copied without modification.
Wallet payloads live in `src/supershare/payments/mod.rs`; the desktop generates their QR codes locally.

Brand paths are adapted from [Simple Icons](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons) ([CC0](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/blob/develop/LICENSE.md)); colored backgrounds are WindShare presentation.
